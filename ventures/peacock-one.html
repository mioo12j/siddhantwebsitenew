The Idea

Every Business Tool.
One Peacock.

Most businesses juggle five different tools — one for billing, one for stock, one for accounts, one for GST, one for reports. Peacock One folds all of it into a single platform, so the data flows once and works everywhere. Named after India's national bird: many feathers, one body.

01

One-Stop Solution

Sales, purchases, stock, accounts, compliance — everything connected, nothing re-entered. One login runs the entire business.

02

GST-First Design

GST isn't an add-on — it's built into the core. Every invoice, credit note, and ledger entry is compliance-aware from the start.

03

For Any Business

From a single shop to a growing enterprise — Peacock One scales with the business, not against it.
