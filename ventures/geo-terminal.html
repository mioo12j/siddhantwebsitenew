The Idea

The Whole World,
On One Command Line

Traders watch markets. Analysts watch the news. Geo Terminal watches both — and shows how they move each other. Live prices, charts, and a geopolitical event feed in one workspace, navigable entirely from the keyboard. Type a command, get an answer. No tabs, no noise, no waiting.

Capabilities

Everything a Desk
Needs to Decide

Live Markets

Real-time quotes, watchlists, and movers across equities, FX, commodities, and crypto — streaming, not stale.

Charting & Analytics

Candlesticks, indicators, and historical depth — technical analysis built into every symbol you pull up.

Geopolitical Feed

A curated stream of world events — conflicts, policy, energy, trade — tagged and linked to the assets they move.

Command Bar

Drive the whole terminal from the keyboard. Type a ticker, a country, a command — the screen reconfigures instantly.

Multi-Panel Workspace

Lay out the panels you need — markets, charts, news, monitors — and save workspaces for every kind of session.

Alerts & Monitoring

Set conditions on prices and events; Geo Terminal pings you the moment the world changes in a way you care about.
