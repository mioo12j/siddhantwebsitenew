Chapter 01 — Who I Am

The Person
Behind the Name

◆

The most honest version of a person lives in what they choose to create — and what they choose to question. I have tried to build my life around both.

I am Siddhant Kumar: an author, a poet, a software developer, a founder, and a student. My days are spent at the intersection of literature and logic — where a poem can carry the weight of an idea, and a line of code can carry the weight of a promise.

I write because writing is how I understand things honestly. I build software because building is how I test whether an idea survives contact with reality. I study history and geopolitics because perspective is the difference between opinion and judgement. None of these are separate lives; they are one curiosity wearing different clothes.

Discipline, to me, has never been a constraint. It is the structure that sets creativity free — the quiet hours, the daily pages, the projects finished rather than merely begun. I believe meaningful work is not an accident. It is a habit.

"Writing has always been my way of understanding the world more honestly."

And because many people share my name: this website is the home of the Siddhant Kumar who writes war poetry, founds student platforms, and builds terminals and robots — so that when you look for me, you find me.

  • I

    The Writer

    Words are my first language. Poetry taught me the economy of it — that one word, placed right, can hold what a paragraph cannot.

  • II

    The Builder

    I approach code the way I approach prose — with intention, structure, and care for the person on the other side of it.

  • III

    The Student of History

    Defence, geopolitics, strategy — I study how nations remember and forget, because memory is where my poems begin.

  • IV

    The Founder

    Ideas deserve institutions. I start things — platforms, software, projects — and stay long enough to make them real.

Who is Siddhant Kumar?

Siddhant Kumar is an Indian author, poet, software developer and founder from Delhi. He wrote Guardians in the Gale: The Unsung Breath of Heroes (2025), a poetry collection honouring soldiers and military families, founded the student platform Inspire Talent Hub, and builds software including the Peacock One ERP and the Geo Terminal intelligence platform. He studies at Army Public School, Dhaula Kuan, and is an official India Book of Records 2027 record holder.

Which Siddhant Kumar is this website about?

Many people share the name Siddhant Kumar. This website is the official home of the Siddhant Kumar who is the author of Guardians in the Gale, an India Book of Records 2027 record holder, a student at Army Public School Dhaula Kuan in Delhi, the founder of Inspire Talent Hub, and the developer behind Peacock One and Geo Terminal. His work has been appreciated by Chief Ministers, Governors, the Chief of Defence Staff, chiefs of the Indian Armed Forces, foreign ambassadors, Harvard University professors and Buckingham Palace.

What books has Siddhant Kumar written?

His published debut is Guardians in the Gale: The Unsung Breath of Heroes (2025), a collection of 21 poems honouring soldiers, intelligence personnel and military families — officially inaugurated by the Chief Minister of Uttarakhand and now an India Book of Records 2027 record-holding work. His second collection, In the Absence of Gunfire, explores the emotional aftermath of conflict and is in final review. A third work is currently being drafted.

What is Siddhant Kumar building?

Three ventures: Inspire Talent Hub, a national competition and mentorship platform for students; Peacock One, a complete ERP ecosystem for businesses; and Geo Terminal, an intelligence platform combining financial markets, geopolitics and strategic analysis. Alongside these he has built more than 100 IoT systems and 25+ AI projects, including Rover, a 3D-printed AI robot dog.
