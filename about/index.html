Who I Am

I am Siddhant Kumar — a student, writer, poet, and builder from Delhi, India. I study at Army Public School, Dhaula Kuan in New Delhi, a school whose motto of discipline has shaped almost everything about how I work. My days are divided between reading, writing, studying, and building — and this website exists to hold all of that work in one place.

Everything I do grows from a single habit: curiosity. I write because I enjoy understanding ideas. I build software because I enjoy solving problems. I study military history and geopolitics because I want perspective. None of these pursuits are separate careers; they are the same instinct pointed in different directions.

The Writing

In February 2025 I published my first book, Guardians in the Gale: The Unsung Breath of Heroes — a collection of 21 poems about soldiers, spies, and the families who wait for them. The book tries to do one thing honestly: convert the abstract vocabulary of service and sacrifice back into individual human beings.

The book travelled further than I expected. Letters of appreciation arrived from the Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh, the Chief of the Air Staff of the Indian Air Force, the Governors of Arunachal Pradesh and Gujarat, senior commanders across the Indian Army and the Armed Forces Medical Services, the office of the Chief of Defence Staff, the Rajya Sabha Secretariat, professors at Harvard University, and the Royal Correspondence office at Buckingham Palace. Scans of these letters are published on the testimonials section of this site so that anyone can verify them.

My second collection, In the Absence of Gunfire, examines what conflict leaves behind — it is currently in final review for publication. A third work is in the drafting phase.

Alongside the books, I write The Journal — a growing library of long-form essays on Indian military history, strategic affairs, and geopolitics, from the Battle of Saragarhi to semiconductor supply chains. Every essay is researched, sourced, and written by me.

The Building

Writing is half of my work; the other half is engineering. I taught myself web development, and that skill grew into real products and ventures:

  • Peacock One — a one-stop business ERP with GST filing, invoicing, inventory, and accounting.
  • Geo Terminal — a Bloomberg-style, keyboard-driven terminal that fuses live market data with world events.
  • Inspire Talent Hub — a national competition platform for students that I founded, combining competitions, mentorship, and merit-based recognition.
  • Rover — a 3D-printed, AI-powered quadruped robot dog, built from the ground up.
  • A catalogue of 100+ IoT projects and 25+ AI projects — sensors, automation, machine learning, and embedded systems.

I have also worked professionally on design and development — overseeing graphics, websites, and digital communication for ARRAYS INGENIERIA PRIVATE LIMITED, and building brand and visual assets for Sri Harghar Solar Pvt Ltd and NS Enterprises.

The Learning

I treat courses, competitions, and conferences as part of the same discipline as writing and building. I completed HarvardX's "Rhetoric: The Art of Persuasive Writing and Public Speaking" (2024) and "Justice" by Michael J. Sandel (2024). I placed 2nd in the National G20 Discourse Competition (2023), took first places in rhetoric and poem writing at national student competitions (2024), and have participated in the Annual Indian Military Heritage Festival, INTACH's Filmit competition, and national student conferences.

Not that I have done everything. Not that I know everything. But I genuinely care about learning, creating, writing, building, and contributing.
