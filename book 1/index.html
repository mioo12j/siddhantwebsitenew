The Book

Not the Glory of War —
Its Quiet, Unseen Truths

◆

War is not just the clash of arms, nor is patriotism a flag fluttering in the wind. They are deeply human contradictions, where love meets loss, courage walks with fear, and duty speaks through silence.

Guardians in the Gale is a collection of 21 poems that reflect not on the glory of war, but on its quiet, often unseen truths — a soldier's final letter, a mother's folded grief, a spy's lonely mission. These verses reach beyond action to explore the soul of service.

From snow-laced borders to silent waiting rooms, each poem captures not just how soldiers die, but how they live, love, and carry the weight of a nation's promise. Writing from the perspective of a fading hero, a grieving wife, or even the flag itself, this collection dares to feel war without armour, and patriotism without noise.

"These verses reach beyond action to explore the soul of service."

Dedication

To the guardians of our freedom —

who brave the fiercest storms and harshest terrains,
whose sacrifices light the path toward our nation's future.

To the soldiers who stand unwavering at the borders,
their resolve unbroken, their courage unmatched,
ready to lay down their lives for the soil they hold sacred.

To the mothers, fathers, children, and loved ones
who silently bear the weight of separation —
their sacrifices no less noble than those on the frontlines.

And to the unsung heroes whose names remain unknown,
whose stories are etched not in books,
but in the very heartbeat of a free nation —

this book is for you.

Preface

From the Author

◆

"Until those who are silenced find their voice, the world will only hear the victor's version of the story."

History is often told by those who prevailed. Yet, in the shadows of every triumph lie the stories of countless unsung heroes — soldiers, freedom fighters, and their families — whose sacrifices made those victories possible.

Growing up in a family deeply rooted in the armed forces, I have always been moved by the strength, discipline, and sacrifices of soldiers who dedicate their lives to the nation. These are individuals who do not fight for glory or recognition, but for the enduring love of their country. Their stories — often overshadowed by the larger narratives of war — deserve to be remembered, cherished, and passed on.

May these words carry the voices of those who stood in the gale — guarding not just our borders, but the very dreams of a nation.

Questions

Before You Ask

◆

What is Guardians in the Gale about?

Guardians in the Gale is a collection of 21 poems by Siddhant Kumar that reflect on the quiet, unseen truths of war — honouring the Indian Armed Forces, covert operatives, and the families who wait at home.

Who wrote Guardians in the Gale?

The book was written by the young Indian poet and author Siddhant Kumar, an India Book of Records 2027 official record holder, whose writing focuses on national duty, human longing, and the silent sacrifices of the military.

What records and recognitions has the book received?

It is an India Book of Records 2027 record-holding work, was officially inaugurated by Shri Pushkar Singh Dhami, Hon'ble Chief Minister of Uttarakhand, and has received letters of appreciation from Chief Ministers, Governors, the Chief of Defence Staff, the Chief of the Air Staff, senior commanders of the Armed Forces, Harvard professors and Buckingham Palace.

How many poems are in the book?

The collection contains 21 poems, each tied to a real chapter of Indian military history — from the 1967 Nathu La clashes and the 1971 war to Kargil, 26/11, and the silent world of special forces and intelligence.

Where can I buy Guardians in the Gale?

The book is available online through Amazon India.
