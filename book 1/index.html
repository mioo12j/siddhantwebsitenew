About the Book

War is not just the clash of arms, nor is patriotism a flag fluttering in the wind. They are deeply human contradictions, where love meets loss, courage walks with fear, and duty speaks through silence.

Guardians in the Gale is a collection of 21 poems that reflect not on the glory of war, but on its quiet, often unseen truths—a soldier's final letter, a mother's folded grief, a spy's lonely mission. These verses reach beyond action to explore the soul of service.

From snow-laced borders to silent waiting rooms, each poem captures not just how soldiers die, but how they live, love, and carry the weight of a nation's promise. Writing from the perspective of a fading hero, a grieving wife, or even the flag itself, this collection dares to feel war without armour, and patriotism without noise.

Guardians in the Gale: The Unsung Breath of Heroes is a poignant collection of 21 poems that delve into the lives, sacrifices, and indomitable spirit of those who safeguard freedom. Each poem offers a profound exploration of soldiers, spies, and their families, capturing their emotions, silent struggles, and unwavering resolve. With vivid imagery and philosophical depth, the collection pays homage to sacrifices that often go unnoticed yet form the backbone of a nation's sovereignty. This book is a tribute to the courage and resilience of those who serve selflessly, ensuring peace and liberty for generations to come.

Dedication

To the guardians of our freedom—

who brave the fiercest storms and harshest terrains,
whose sacrifices light the path toward our nation's future.

To the soldiers who stand unwavering at the borders,
their resolve unbroken, their courage unmatched,
ready to lay down their lives for the soil they hold sacred.

To the mothers, fathers, children, and loved ones
who silently bear the weight of separation—
their sacrifices no less noble than those on the frontlines.

And to the unsung heroes whose names remain unknown,
whose stories are etched not in books,
but in the very heartbeat of a free nation—

this book is for you.

Preface

From the Author

"Until those who are silenced find their voice, the world will only hear the victor's version of the story."

History is often told by those who prevailed. Yet, in the shadows of every triumph lie the stories of countless unsung heroes—soldiers, freedom fighters, and their families—whose sacrifices made those victories possible.

This book, Guardians in the Gale: The Unsung Breath of Heroes, is my attempt to give voice to these silent warriors and illuminate the profound cost of the freedoms we so often take for granted.

Growing up in a family deeply rooted in the armed forces, I have always been moved by the strength, discipline, and sacrifices of soldiers who dedicate their lives to the nation. These are individuals who do not fight for glory or recognition, but for the enduring love of their country. Their stories—often overshadowed by the larger narratives of war—deserve to be remembered, cherished, and passed on to future generations.

This collection of 21 poems seeks to step into the lives of these guardians, capturing their emotions, struggles, and unwavering resolve. Each verse reflects the price paid for freedom, the moments of triumph and despair, and the spirit of those who gave everything so that others might live in peace.

To every reader who picks up this book, I hope it inspires a deeper appreciation for the silent sacrifices made by our armed forces. May these words carry the voices of those who stood in the gale—guarding not just our borders, but the very dreams of a nation.

The Author

Siddhant Kumar

SK

Siddhant Kumar is a poet whose words emerge from the quiet spaces between duty and love, between service and longing.

Growing up in a family deeply rooted in the armed forces, Siddhant has always been drawn to the stories that live in the margins of history—stories of courage whispered in letters never sent, of mothers holding vigils by lamplit windows, of soldiers whose names the world never learned to say.

With Guardians in the Gale, his debut poetry collection, Siddhant gives voice to the unsung—those who guard not just borders but the very dreams of a nation. His writing fuses vivid imagery with philosophical depth, creating verses that feel both ancient and urgently contemporary.

He writes not for glory, but for remembrance. Not for applause, but for the quiet acknowledgment that every free moment owes its existence to a guardian who stood in the gale.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the book Guardians in the Gale about?

Guardians in the Gale is a collection of 21 patriotic poems by Siddhant Kumar that reflect on the quiet, unseen truths of war — honouring the Indian Armed Forces, covert operatives, and the families who wait at home.

Who wrote Guardians in the Gale?

The book was written by the young Indian poet and author Siddhant Kumar, whose writing focuses on national duty, human longing, and the silent sacrifices of the military.

What are the main themes of the collection?

The primary themes are patriotic duty, silent sacrifice, the human cost of war, the resilience of military families, and the enduring legacy of those who protect the nation.

How many poems are in the book?

The collection contains 21 poems, each tied to a real chapter of Indian military history — from the 1967 Nathu La clashes and the 1971 war to Kargil, 26/11, and the silent world of special forces and intelligence.
