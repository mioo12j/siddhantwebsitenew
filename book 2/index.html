About the Collection

What War Leaves Behind

In the Absence of Gunfire is a collection of 21 poems examining the profound echoes of conflict. These verses turn away from the noise of the front line and toward the stillness that follows — the empty chair at the table, the folded flag, the letters read and re-read.

It is a meditation on the cost of peace and the people who quietly pay it: the soldiers who carry the war home inside them, and the families who hold the line on the other side of the silence.

Peace is a beautiful thing to hold, Yet its creation is harsh and cold.

Like its predecessor, the book is a handcrafted tribute — written with the same care, discipline, and reverence for those who serve beyond the spotlight.
